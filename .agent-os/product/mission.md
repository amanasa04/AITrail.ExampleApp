# Product Mission

## Pitch

Dynamic Team Manager is an internal team administration tool that helps managers and operations teams maintain accurate employee-to-team assignments by replacing spreadsheets with a centralized, searchable system and built-in reporting.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- People Managers and Team Leads: Create, update, and review team membership in one place.
- HR and People Operations: Ensure team structures remain accurate for org reporting and staffing workflows.
- Engineering and Operations Admins: Maintain system integrity, fix data issues, and support downstream integrations.

### User Personas

**Team Manager** (28-55 years old)
- **Role:** People Manager
- **Context:** Managing team changes during hiring, transfers, and reorganizations.
- **Pain Points:** Spreadsheet version conflicts, stale membership records.
- **Goals:** Update team assignments quickly, trust that reporting data is current.

**HR Operations Specialist** (25-50 years old)
- **Role:** People Operations Analyst
- **Context:** Supporting workforce reporting and organizational data quality.
- **Pain Points:** Inconsistent team mapping across source files, heavy manual cleanup.
- **Goals:** Keep assignments accurate, reduce reconciliation work for reporting cycles.

## The Problem

### Spreadsheet-Driven Team Management

Team membership is often maintained in disconnected spreadsheets, creating duplicated effort and conflicting versions. Managers can spend 1-2 hours per week reconciling updates.

**Our Solution:** Provide a centralized application for creating, updating, and viewing teams with shared source-of-truth records.

### Low Confidence in Team Data

When team assignments are manually maintained, reporting and integrations can receive stale or mismatched records. Even a 5-10% error rate can break downstream workflows.

**Our Solution:** Add controlled team membership updates, searchable views, and clear ownership for data maintenance.

### Slow Reporting and Integration Readiness

Operational teams need basic team composition reporting and clean data exports, but spreadsheet aggregation causes delays and frequent corrections.

**Our Solution:** Include built-in basic reporting and integration-ready data outputs from a single managed dataset.

## Differentiators

### Purpose-Built for Team Membership

Unlike generic spreadsheets, we provide dedicated team and membership workflows with search and filtering. This results in faster updates and less manual reconciliation.

### Integration-Ready Accuracy

Unlike ad hoc files that require cleanup before handoff, we provide consistent structured records and reporting views. This results in higher trust for downstream reporting and integrations.

### Internal Tooling Aligned to Enterprise Stack

Unlike standalone low-code solutions, we provide a tool aligned with the internal React, .NET, SQL Server, and enterprise authentication stack. This results in easier governance, support, and long-term maintainability.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Team CRUD Management:** Create, rename, and retire teams from a single interface.
- **Employee-to-Team Tagging:** Assign and reassign employees to teams with clear ownership.
- **Team Membership Views:** Display current team rosters with key employee metadata.
- **Search Across Teams and Members:** Find teams or employees quickly using keyword search.
- **Filterable Membership Lists:** Narrow results by manager, location, department, and status.
- **Change-Safe Updates:** Reduce accidental data loss through controlled update workflows.

### Collaboration Features

- **Basic Team Reporting:** Provide summary metrics such as team size and composition.
- **Integration Export Support:** Enable downstream systems to consume accurate team data.
- **Role-Aware Access Patterns:** Support manager, HR, and admin responsibilities clearly.
- **Operational Audit Signals:** Track key updates for troubleshooting and accountability.
