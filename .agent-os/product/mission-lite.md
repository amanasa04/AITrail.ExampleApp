# Product Mission (Lite)

Dynamic Team Manager is an internal tool that helps managers and operations teams create, update, and view employee teams in one place instead of maintaining spreadsheets.

The product serves managers, HR and People Operations, and admins who need accurate team assignment data for daily operations. It combines team tagging, search and filter workflows, and basic reporting so team information stays current and trustworthy for downstream integrations. Its primary differentiator is centralized, integration-ready data quality within an enterprise-approved internal stack.
